\title{A Decoder-Only Jacobian Lens for Speech-to-Text Models}

\author{
\IEEEauthorblockN{Januda Lelwala, Anas Hussaindeen, Chandupa Ambepitiya, and Dewmike Amarasinghe}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Department of Computer Science and Engineering\\
University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka}
}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The Jacobian lens identifies representations a language model is poised to verbalize by averaging causal maps from a layer's residual stream to the final pre-logit state. Encoder--decoder speech recognizers split that residual across two stacks: an encoder that is never unembedded and a decoder that is. We show that a pooled encoder-to-decoder transport is not this object---it destroys the causal \((t,t')\) structure and leaves the unembedding off-manifold. We instead fit one Jacobian per decoder block,
\(J_\ell=\mathbb{E}_{t,\,t'\ge t}[\partial h_{\mathrm{final},t'}/\partial h_{\ell,t}]\),
with Hutchinson probes, and read out \(\operatorname{softmax}(E J_\ell h_{\ell,t})\) through Whisper's own tied projection. The construction is implemented in ECHO. On a linear stub decoder the estimator recovers the closed form \(J=2M/(T+1)\) to relative Frobenius error \(<0.1\); a Whisper-base fit yields six \(512\times 512\) maps. We specify a reading protocol for the resulting \((\text{position}\times\text{layer})\) grid and leave a workspace-band census as future work.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
speech recognition, mechanistic interpretability, Jacobian lens, Whisper, encoder--decoder Transformers

\section{Introduction}

Whisper~\cite{radford2023whisper} and related encoder--decoder ASR models transcribe by unembedding a decoder residual stream that has attended to a frozen acoustic encoder. Failures such as silence-induced repetition, homophone substitution, and early cutoff are well known, yet the decoder state that is about to become the next token is rarely inspected in vocabulary space.

Gurnee et~al.~\cite{gurnee2026workspace} give language models a tool for that inspection: the Jacobian lens averages \(\partial h_{\mathrm{final},t'}/\partial h_{\ell,t}\) over causally allowed pairs \(t'\ge t\), then reads the transported residual with the model's unembedding. The ranked tokens are what the model is \emph{poised to verbalize}. The construction assumes a single residual stream that ends in an unembedding. Speech-to-text models do not: the encoder never sees that unembedding.

This paper ports the estimator to the stream that does. We fit decoder-only Jacobians for seq2seq ASR, estimate them with Hutchinson probes~\cite{hutchinson1989trace} so the encoder can sit outside the autograd graph, and ship fit/apply as jobs in ECHO, an audio interpretability workbench in the spirit of LIT~\cite{tenney2020lit}. We also record why a pooled encoder transport---the obvious first attempt---is a different, weaker object. We do not claim that Whisper has a global workspace; we claim that if one wants the LLM lens, one must fit it on the decoder.

\section{Related Work}

The logit lens~\cite{nostalgebraist2020logitlens} applies the unembedding to intermediate residuals (identity transport). The tuned lens~\cite{belrose2023tuned} learns an affine map to the final layer. The Jacobian lens~\cite{gurnee2026workspace} is the average \emph{derivative} of the true residual-to-final map. We copy that estimator, not the LLM write/hold/ablate suite.

DecoderLens~\cite{langedijk2024decoderlens} feeds intermediate \emph{encoder} layers to a frozen decoder's cross-attention so the decoder can verbalize encoder states. Langedijk et~al.\ already run this on Whisper transcription: early encoder layers emit empty or repetitive strings; later ones approximate the transcript. Complementary: they interpret the encoder \emph{through} the decoder; we interpret the decoder \emph{with} its own Jacobian. Linear probes~\cite{alain2017probes,hewitt2019structural,belinkov2022probing} test linearly available features but are not maps onto the model's unembedding.

ECHO is the audio analogue of LIT~\cite{tenney2020lit}: attention, saliency, embeddings, and perturbations on Whisper and wav2vec~2.0~\cite{baevski2020wav2vec2}. Attention says where the decoder looked in the audio; saliency says which waveform regions moved a scalar. Neither says what the decoder is poised to say. CTC models have no decoder residual to unembed and are excluded.

\section{Method}

\subsection{Substrate}

Whisper is an encoder--decoder Transformer~\cite{vaswani2017attention}. A 30\,s log-Mel spectrogram (80 bins \(\times\) 3000 frames on the base model) is downsampled by a stride-2 convolution to \(n_{\mathrm{audio}}=1500\) encoder positions of width \(d=512\). The decoder, at each transcript position \(t\le 448\), attends causally to its prefix and, via cross-attention, to all 1500 encoder frames, then unembeds with a tied projection \(E\in\mathbb{R}^{V\times d}\) (\(V=51865\)). We write \(h_{\ell,t}\in\mathbb{R}^d\) for the residual at decoder block \(\ell\) and position \(t\), and \(h_{\mathrm{final},t'}\) for the post-final-LayerNorm state that \(E\) consumes.

Current HuggingFace Whisper records decoder hidden states from each decoder block only. The embedding output is not in that tuple; \(h_{\mathrm{final}}\) is a distinct tensor (final LayerNorm of the last block). The lens therefore has \emph{one map per decoder block}: six on Whisper-base, matching a fitted artifact on this codebase. The encoder is computed once without gradients and is never differentiated.

\subsection{Estimator}

Following~\cite{gurnee2026workspace}, the lens at block \(\ell\) is
\begin{equation}
J_\ell \;=\;
\mathbb{E}_{t,\,t'\ge t,\,\text{clip}}
\!\left[
\frac{\partial h_{\mathrm{final},t'}}{\partial h_{\ell,t}}
\right]
\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}.
\label{eq:J}
\end{equation}
The model is frozen. There is no optimizer and no learned baseline. Fit is teacher-forced: labels are shifted right past the decoder-start token so all \(T\) positions run in one pass.

Exact Jacobians are unnecessary. For a Rademacher probe \(r\in\{\pm 1\}^d\), the scalar \(\sum_{t'}\langle h_{\mathrm{final},t'},r\rangle\) has vector--Jacobian product
\(g_t=\sum_{t'\ge t}J_\ell(t\!\to\! t')^\top r\)
at every source \(t\) at once, because the causal mask zeroes \(t>t'\). The identity \(\mathbb{E}[rr^\top]=I\) then gives
\begin{equation}
\widehat{J}_\ell
\;=\;
\frac{1}{NP\cdot T(T+1)/2}
\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sum_{p=1}^{P}
r^{(p)}\Bigl(\sum_t g_t^{(n,p)}\Bigr)^\top,
\label{eq:hutch}
\end{equation}
with \(N\) clips and \(P\) probes~\cite{hutchinson1989trace}. Product defaults are \(P=4\) (range \(1\)--\(32\)) and \(N\in[2,1000]\). Audio is truncated at 30\,s (cap 60\,s); Whisper still pads Mel to 30\,s.

\subsection{Readout}

Apply re-runs the decoder. Positions come from the model's generation routine (Whisper's decoding mixin) or from a provided transcript. A teacher-forced pass collects \(h_{\ell,t}\). Each cell is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{lens}(h_{\ell,t})
\;=\;
\operatorname{softmax}\bigl(E\,J_\ell h_{\ell,t}\bigr),
\label{eq:lens}
\end{equation}
implemented as \(h J_\ell^\top E^\top\). Whisper's final LayerNorm is already inside \(h_{\mathrm{final}}\), so it is absorbed into \(J_\ell\); we do not apply an extra RMS-norm (unlike~\cite{gurnee2026workspace}). Top-\(k\) (default \(k=5\)) ranks logits; the softmax is for display, not calibrated confidence. Neighboring cells are not a phrase.

The readout axis is transcript position, not audio time. Which encoder frame supports a token remains a cross-attention question.

\subsection{Why encoder transports fail}

An earlier design fitted a global map from the mean-pooled encoder state to the mean-pooled decoder state and applied it to 0.3\,s encoder buckets (Table~\ref{tab:redesign}). Differentiating after pooling erases \((t,t')\) alignment; the linearization is around clip means but is applied locally; the unembedding is used off-manifold with the constant dropped. That object is not~\eqref{eq:J}. CTC support came for free (the encoder is the only stream) and disappeared with the redesign: a CTC head never verbalizes through a decoder workspace.

\section{Implementation and verification}

ECHO stores a format-v2 artifact: architecture \emph{decoder}, method Hutchinson-decoder-VJP, one square \(d\times d\) matrix per recorded decoder block, bound to a model revision. Apply refuses a mismatch. Only seq2seq ASR adapters expose the architecture; wav2vec~2.0 returns none. Fig.~\ref{fig:arch} sketches the data flow. A production fit on this codebase uses Whisper-base, \(N=1000\) transcripts, \(P=4\), and writes six \(512\times 512\) maps.

\paragraph{Linear closed form.}
A unit-test stub uses a position-wise linear decoder \(h_{\mathrm{final},t}=M h_{\ell,t}\) with no mixing across positions. Then \(\partial h_{\mathrm{final},t'}/\partial h_{\ell,t}\) is \(M\) on the diagonal \(t'=t\) and zero otherwise, so the triangular mean in~\eqref{eq:J} is exactly \(2M/(T+1)\). With \(P=512\) Rademacher probes the fitted map matches this matrix to relative Frobenius error below \(0.1\). Apply on the same stub matches an independent recomputation of \((J h)E^\top\) at a held-out cell.

These tests check the estimator and the readout identity. They are not a Whisper workspace census.

\section{Reading the grid}

Fig.~\ref{fig:grid} is the contract of one apply job: rows are decoder blocks (UI ``Layer \(\ell{+}1\)'' for 0-based \(\ell\)), columns are decoder input tokens, each cell is a top-\(k\) ranking. The following readings are \emph{affordances of that contract}, not measured effects. We state them so the tool can be used without over-interpreting a cell.

\textbf{Start-of-transcript.}
The first decoder input is the start token. No transcribed word is in the prefix. The residual still contains that token's embedding and position; audio enters only through cross-attention. The cell is the closest available reading of what the audio wrote into the decoder before any transcript language model context.

\textbf{Depth trajectory.}
A column can be read from early blocks (often frequent-token geometry) toward later blocks (closer to the emitted word). Where a content word first enters the top-\(k\) is a candidate crystallization depth. A column that never ranks the emitted word is a candidate for transcription against the decoder's internal ranking.

\textbf{Prior versus evidence.}
Agreement of mid and late blocks on a collocation is consistent with a language-model prior. A late-block flip to an acoustically specific word is consistent with audio-driven selection. This is a comparison of rankings, not a causal mediation test.

\textbf{Ambiguity, loops, endpoints.}
A wide top-\(k\) (their/there/they're) is consistent with under-determined acoustics. Rankings of a repetition token before generation emits it would be a leading indicator of Whisper's silence loop, if observed. Drift toward end-of-text or trained closers while speech continues would be an early-cutoff signature, if observed.

A layer-wise top-1 agreement curve---the speech analogue of the LLM workspace band~\cite{gurnee2026workspace}---is not reported here.

\section{Limitations}

\(J_\ell\) is a first-order average: trust rankings more than scores, and expect error far from the fit distribution. Averaging over pairs erases which audio frame a token reads. Fit is teacher-forced, so \(J\) reflects ``given this prefix,'' not free-running dynamics. \(P=4\) leaves probe noise; the closed-form test used \(P=512\). Frequent tokens dominate geometry. The artifact is revision-bound. Write-mode interventions from~\cite{gurnee2026workspace} are unimplemented. Layer count follows whatever HuggingFace records as decoder hidden states; it is six on current Whisper-base, not an embedding-plus-blocks stack.

\section{Conclusion}

The Jacobian lens is a statement about a residual that is about to be unembedded. In encoder--decoder ASR that residual is the decoder's. Fitted there, with causal averaging and the model's own \(E\), it is the same estimator as in~\cite{gurnee2026workspace} and is already a job in ECHO. Fitted on pooled encoder states, it is not a lens. The measurements that would make the speech case as strong as the LLM case---workspace-band statistics, multi-size Whisper, and a write mode---should be taken on this decoder construction.
